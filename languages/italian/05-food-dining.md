# Lesson 5: Food & Dining

**Goal:** Order confidently at a restaurant or café, handle the bill, and state dietary needs.

## At the restaurant

| Italian | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Un tavolo per due, per favore | oon TAH-voh-loh pehr DOO-eh | A table for two, please |
| Il menù, per favore | eel meh-NOO | The menu, please |
| Vorrei... | vohr-REH-ee | I would like... |
| Cosa mi consiglia? | KOH-zah mee kohn-SEE-lyah | What do you recommend? |
| Per me... | pehr meh | For me... (ordering) |
| Da bere? | dah BEH-reh | To drink? |
| Il conto, per favore | eel KOHN-toh | The bill, please |
| È incluso il servizio? | eh een-KLOO-zoh eel sehr-VEE-tsyoh | Is service included? |
| Buon appetito! | bwohn ahp-peh-TEE-toh | Enjoy your meal! |

## Example dialogue

| Italian | English |
|---|---|
| Cameriere: Buonasera! Un tavolo? | Waiter: Good evening! A table? |
| Tu: Sì, per due, per favore. | You: Yes, for two, please. |
| Cameriere: Ecco il menù. Cosa prendete? | Waiter: Here's the menu. What will you have? |
| Tu: Vorrei una pizza margherita e un'acqua naturale. | You: I'd like a margherita pizza and a still water. |
| Cameriere: Perfetto. Qualcos'altro? | Waiter: Perfect. Anything else? |
| Tu: No, grazie. Il conto, per favore, alla fine. | You: No, thanks. The bill, please, at the end. |
| Cameriere: Certo, buon appetito! | Waiter: Of course, enjoy your meal! |

## Practice

1. Act out the full dialogue, playing both the waiter and customer roles.
2. Order your actual favorite meal in Italian using "Vorrei...".
3. Say "Sono allergico/a a..." with a real allergy (or made-up one for practice).
4. Practice asking "Cosa mi consiglia?" and improvise a waiter's answer.
