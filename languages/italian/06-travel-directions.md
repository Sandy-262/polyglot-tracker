# Lesson 6: Travel & Directions

**Goal:** Navigate a city, use transport, and check into a hotel without panicking.

## Getting around

| Italian | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| il treno | eel TREH-noh | the train |
| l'autobus | low-TOH-boos | the bus |
| la metro | lah MEH-troh | the metro |
| il taxi | eel TAHK-see | the taxi |
| la stazione | lah stah-TSYOH-neh | the station |
| l'aeroporto | lah-eh-roh-POHR-toh | the airport |

## Direction words

| Italian | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| a destra | ah DEHS-trah | to the right |
| a sinistra | ah see-NEES-trah | to the left |
| sempre dritto | SEHM-preh DREET-toh | straight ahead |
| vicino | vee-CHEE-noh | near |
| lontano | lohn-TAH-noh | far |
| qui / qua | kwee / kwah | here |
| lì / là | lee / lah | there |
| all'angolo | ahl-LAHN-goh-loh | at the corner |

## Asking for directions

| Italian | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Scusi, dov'è...? | SKOO-zee, doh-VEH | Excuse me, where is...? |
| Come arrivo a...? | KOH-meh ahr-REE-voh ah | How do I get to...? |
| È lontano da qui? | eh lohn-TAH-noh dah kwee | Is it far from here? |
| Quanto tempo ci vuole? | KWAHN-toh TEHM-poh chee VWOH-leh | How long does it take? |
| Mi sono perso/a | mee SOH-noh PEHR-soh/sah | I'm lost |

## Example dialogue

| Italian | English |
|---|---|
| Tu: Scusi, come arrivo alla stazione? | You: Excuse me, how do I get to the station? |
| Passante: Vada sempre dritto, poi a destra. | Passerby: Go straight ahead, then right. |
| Tu: È lontano da qui? | You: Is it far from here? |
| Passante: No, cinque minuti a piedi. | Passerby: No, five minutes on foot. |
| Tu: Grazie mille! | You: Thanks a lot! |
| Passante: Prego, buon viaggio! | Passerby: You're welcome, safe travels! |

## Practice

1. Act out the directions dialogue, then reverse roles.
2. Describe the route from your home to the nearest shop in Italian, using at least 3 direction words.
3. Practice "Ho una prenotazione" and improvise what a hotel clerk might ask next.
4. Say "Mi sono perso/a" and then ask for help finding "la stazione".
