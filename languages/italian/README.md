# 🇮🇹 Italian

Italian is one of the most rewarding "bonus" languages if you're also learning Spanish or French — the vocabulary overlap is huge, the spelling is almost perfectly phonetic (what you see is what you say), and it opens up a famously vivid food, music, and travel culture. It's also a confidence-builder: because pronunciation is so consistent, you'll sound convincing much faster than in English or French.

## Suggested 4-week plan

| Week | Lessons | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01, 02 | Get the sounds right, learn to greet and introduce yourself |
| 2 | 03, 04 | Numbers/time + a survival phrase toolkit |
| 3 | 05, 06 | Food and travel — the two situations you'll actually use most |
| 4 | 07, 08 | Grammar to build your own sentences, then tie it all together |

After week 4, revisit **Lesson 8** weekly — read the dialogue out loud, then improvise your own version. Speaking out loud, even alone, matters more than adding new vocabulary at this stage.
