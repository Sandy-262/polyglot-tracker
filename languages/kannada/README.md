# Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)

Kannada is a Dravidian language spoken by roughly 45 million people, mainly in the Indian state of Karnataka. Coming from Italian, Spanish, or French, the two biggest adjustments are:

- **A completely different script** — an abugida where consonants carry an inherent vowel, not the Latin alphabet.
- **SOV word order** (Subject–Object–Verb) instead of SVO — the verb always comes last.

There's no grammatical noun gender to memorize like in Romance languages, which offsets some of the extra effort the script and word order require.

## Suggested study plan

- **Weeks 1–4:** 2 lessons per week, in order (1→8). Each lesson takes 20–40 minutes including the Listen & Practice and Practice sections.
- **Every week:** revisit Lesson 8's dialogue out loud as a 5-minute speaking warm-up, even after you've "finished" it.
- **Ongoing:** recorded audio resources for Kannada are thinner than for European languages in this library, so prioritize real conversation — with Kannada-speaking family/friends if you have them, or via a language exchange app (Tandem, HelloTalk) — over passive listening.
