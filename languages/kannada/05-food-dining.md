# Lesson 5: Food & Dining

**Goal:** Order food, state dietary needs, and handle a basic restaurant interaction.

## Restaurant basics

| Kannada | Romanized | English |
|---|---|---|
| ಹೋಟೆಲ್ | hoTel | restaurant (common usage in Karnataka) |
| ಮೆನು | menu | menu |
| ನನಗೆ ___ ಬೇಕು | nanage ___ beeku | I want ___ |
| ದಯವಿಟ್ಟು ಇದನ್ನು ಕೊಡಿ | dayaviTTu idannu koDi | Please give me this |
| ನೀರು ಬೇಕು | niiru beeku | I need water |
| ಬಿಲ್ ಕೊಡಿ | bill koDi | Please bring the bill |
| ರುಚಿಯಾಗಿದೆ | ruchiyaagide | It's tasty |

## Dietary phrases

| Kannada | Romanized | English |
|---|---|---|
| ನಾನು ಸಸ್ಯಾಹಾರಿ | naanu sasyaahaari | I am vegetarian |
| ನಾನು ಮಾಂಸಾಹಾರ ತಿನ್ನುವುದಿಲ್ಲ | naanu maamsaahaara tinnuvudilla | I don't eat meat |
| ನನಗೆ ___ ಅಲರ್ಜಿ | nanage ___ alarji | I'm allergic to ___ |
| ಕಡಿಮೆ ಖಾರ ಬೇಕು | kaDime khaara beeku | I want it less spicy |
| ಸಕ್ಕರೆ ಇಲ್ಲದೆ | sakkare illade | without sugar |

Note: vegetarian food is the cultural default in much of Karnataka, so "sasyaahaari" is an extremely common and well-understood word — don't hesitate to use it.

## Example dialogue (at a restaurant)

> **Waiter:** ಏನು ಬೇಕು? *(enu beeku?)* — What would you like?
>
> **You:** ಒಂದು ದೋಸೆ ಮತ್ತು ಕಾಫಿ ಬೇಕು. *(ondu doose mattu kaafi beeku.)* — I'd like one dosa and a coffee.
>
> **Waiter:** ಬೇರೆ ಏನಾದರೂ ಬೇಕಾ? *(beere enaadaruu beekaa?)* — Anything else?
>
> **You:** ಇಲ್ಲ, ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು. ಬಿಲ್ ಕೊಡಿ. *(illa, dhanyavaadagaLu. bill koDi.)* — No, thank you. Please bring the bill.

## Practice

1. Order 2 dishes from the table above out loud, using "nanage ___ beeku."
2. Say "naanu sasyaahaari" and "nanage ___ alarji" with a real allergy of yours.
3. Act out the full restaurant dialogue, playing both roles.
4. Ask for the bill and say "ruchiyaagide" afterward.
