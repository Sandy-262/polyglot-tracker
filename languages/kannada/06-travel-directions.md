# Lesson 6: Travel & Directions

**Goal:** Ask for and understand directions, and handle common transport situations.

## Direction words

| Kannada | Romanized | English |
|---|---|---|
| ಎಡ | eDa | left |
| ಬಲ | bala | right |
| ನೇರ | neera | straight |
| ಹತ್ತಿರ | hattira | near |
| ದೂರ | duura | far |
| ಮುಂದೆ | munde | ahead / in front |
| ಹಿಂದೆ | hinde | behind |
| ಇಲ್ಲಿ | illi | here |
| ಅಲ್ಲಿ | alli | there |

## Transport vocabulary

|---|---|---|
| ಆಟೋ | aaTo | auto-rickshaw |
| ಬಸ್ | bus | bus |

## Asking and giving directions

| Kannada | Romanized | English |
|---|---|---|
| ___ ಗೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗೋದು? | ___ ge heege hogodu? | How do I get to ___? |
| ಇಲ್ಲಿಂದ ಎಷ್ಟು ದೂರ? | illinda eshTu duura? | How far from here? |
| ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ | eDakke tirugi | Turn left |
| ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ | balakke tirugi | Turn right |
| ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ | neeravaagi hogi | Go straight |
| ಆಟೋ ಸ್ಟಾಂಡ್ ಎಲ್ಲಿದೆ? | aaTo sTaanD ellide? | Where is the auto-rickshaw stand? |
| ___ ಗೆ ಎಷ್ಟು ಆಗುತ್ತೆ? | ___ ge eshTu aagutte? | How much will it cost to ___? |

## Example dialogue (asking directions)

> **You:** ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ, ರೈಲ್ವೆ ನಿಲ್ದಾಣಕ್ಕೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಹೋಗೋದು?
> *Kshamisi, railve nildaaNakke heege hogodu?*
> (Excuse me, how do I get to the railway station?)
>
> **Stranger:** ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ, ಆಮೇಲೆ ಎಡಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ. ಅದು ಹತ್ತಿರ ಇದೆ.
> *Neeravaagi hogi, aamele eDakke tirugi. Adu hattira ide.*
> (Go straight, then turn left. It's nearby.)
>
> **You:** ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು!
> *DhanyavaadagaLu!*
> (Thank you!)

## Practice

1. Say all 9 direction words out loud from memory.
2. Ask "___ ge heege hogodu?" for 3 different places (airport, station, a friend's house).
3. Role-play the dialogue above with someone, then swap roles.
4. Practice negotiating an auto-rickshaw fare using "___ ge eshTu aagutte?"
