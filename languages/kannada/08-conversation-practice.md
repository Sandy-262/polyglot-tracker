# Lesson 8: Conversation Practice

**Goal:** Combine everything from Lessons 2–6 into one flowing conversation, and set up your ongoing practice routine.

## Full dialogue: meeting someone, chatting, eating, and asking directions

> **A:** ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ! ನೀವು ಹೇಗಿದ್ದೀರಾ?
> *Namaskaara! Niivu heegiddiiraa?*
> (Hello! How are you?)
>
> **B:** ನಾನು ಚೆನ್ನಾಗಿದ್ದೀನಿ, ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು. ನಿಮ್ಮ ಹೆಸರೇನು?
> *Naanu chennaagiddiini, dhanyavaadagaLu. Nimma hesareenu?*
> (I'm fine, thank you. What's your name?)
>
> **A:** ನನ್ನ ಹೆಸರು ಅನಿತಾ. ನೀವು ಎಲ್ಲಿಂದ ಬಂದಿದ್ದೀರಾ?
> *Nanna hesaru Anitaa. Niivu ellinda bandiddiiraa?*
> (My name is Anita. Where are you from?)
>
> **B:** ನಾನು ಅಮೆರಿಕದಿಂದ ಬಂದಿದ್ದೇನೆ. ಇಲ್ಲಿ ಹತ್ತಿರ ಒಳ್ಳೆ ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಇದೆಯಾ?
> *Naanu amerikadinda bandiddeene. Illi hattira oLLe hoTel ideyaa?*
> (I'm from America. Is there a good restaurant nearby?)
>
> **A:** ಹೌದು, ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಹೋಗಿ, ಆಮೇಲೆ ಬಲಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿ. ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಒಳ್ಳೆ ಹೋಟೆಲ್ ಇದೆ.
> *Haudu, neeravaagi hogi, aamele balakke tirugi. Alli ondu oLLe hoTel ide.*
> (Yes, go straight, then turn right. There's a good restaurant there.)
>
> **B:** ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು! ನಾನು ಸಸ್ಯಾಹಾರಿ — ಅಲ್ಲಿ ಇಡ್ಲಿ, ದೋಸೆ ಸಿಗುತ್ತಾ?
> *DhanyavaadagaLu! Naanu sasyaahaari — alli iDli, doose siguttaa?*
> (Thank you! I'm vegetarian — can I get idli, dosa there?)
>
> **A:** ಖಂಡಿತ ಸಿಗುತ್ತೆ! ಮತ್ತೆ ಸಿಗೋಣ.
> *KhanDita sigutte! Matte sigoNa.*
> (Definitely available! See you again.)

## Role-play prompts

Practice these out loud, writing your own lines using vocabulary from Lessons 1–7:

1. **At a bus stand:** Greet a stranger, ask how to get to the airport, and ask how far it is.
2. **At a restaurant:** Order two dishes, state that you're vegetarian, and ask for the bill.
3. **Making a new friend:** Introduce yourself, say where you're from, ask their name, and say "nice to meet you."

## What's next

The single best way to get comfortable speaking Kannada is **real conversation with native speakers** — recorded audio resources for Kannada are much thinner than for Italian, Spanish, or French, so lean on people over content:

- If you know Kannada-speaking family, friends, or colleagues, ask them to run through this lesson's dialogue with you — even 10 minutes a week compounds fast.
- Language exchange apps like **Tandem** and **HelloTalk** have Kannada speakers looking to practice English in exchange for help with Kannada.
- Revisit this lesson weekly as a speaking warm-up, and go back to Lessons 1–7 whenever a word or sound trips you up.
