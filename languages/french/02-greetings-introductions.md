# Lesson 2: Greetings & Introductions

**Goal:** Greet someone, ask how they are, and introduce yourself comfortably.

## Saying hello and goodbye

| French | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Bonjour | bohn-ZHOOR | Hello / Good morning (any time before evening) |
| Bonsoir | bohn-SWAHR | Good evening |
| Salut | sah-LU | Hi / Bye (informal, friends only) |
| Au revoir | oh ruh-VWAHR | Goodbye |
| À bientôt | ah byen-TOH | See you soon |
| À demain | ah duh-MEHN | See you tomorrow |
| Bonne nuit | bun NWEE | Good night (going to bed) |

## Asking how someone is

| French | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Comment allez-vous ? | koh-mahn tah-lay VOO | How are you? (formal) |
| Comment vas-tu ? / Ça va ? | koh-mahn vah-TU / sah VAH | How are you? (informal) |
| Je vais bien, merci | zhuh vay byen mehr-SEE | I'm well, thank you |
| Pas mal | pah MAHL | Not bad |
| Et vous / Et toi ? | ay VOO / ay TWAH | And you? (formal / informal) |

## Introducing yourself

| French | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Je m'appelle... | zhuh mah-PEL | My name is... |
| Comment vous appelez-vous ? | koh-mahn voo zah-play VOO | What is your name? (formal) |
| Comment tu t'appelles ? | koh-mahn tu tah-PEL | What is your name? (informal) |
| Enchanté(e) | ahn-shahn-TAY | Nice to meet you |
| Je suis de... | zhuh swee duh | I am from... |
| Ravi(e) de vous rencontrer | rah-VEE duh voo rahn-kohn-TRAY | Delighted to meet you |

## Formal "vous" vs informal "tu"

French has two ways to say "you": **tu** for friends, family, children, and peers your age; **vous** for strangers, elders, authority figures, or anyone in a professional setting — and always for more than one person. When unsure, start with **vous**; the other person will invite you to use **tu** if appropriate (*"On peut se tutoyer"* — "we can use tu with each other"). Using tu too soon can come across as too familiar or even rude.

## Example dialogue

> **Léa:** Bonjour ! Comment allez-vous ?
> **Marc:** Je vais très bien, merci. Et vous ?
> **Léa:** Pas mal, merci. Comment vous appelez-vous ?
> **Marc:** Je m'appelle Marc. Et vous ?
> **Léa:** Je m'appelle Léa. Enchantée !
> **Marc:** Enchanté ! Au revoir, Léa.
> **Léa:** À bientôt, Marc !

## Practice

1. Read the dialogue above out loud, playing both parts.
2. Introduce yourself in French: name, where you're from, and "enchanté(e)."
3. Decide whether you'd use tu or vous with: a shop clerk, your best friend, your boss, a child.
4. Record yourself asking "Comment allez-vous ?" and answering it.
