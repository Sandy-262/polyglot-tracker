# French (Français)

French is spoken by over 300 million people across France, Canada, Belgium, Switzerland, and much of West/Central Africa, making it one of the most globally useful languages to learn. As a Romance language, it shares a lot of vocabulary and grammar logic with Italian and Spanish — if you're learning those too, French verb patterns and sentence structure will feel familiar, even though the pronunciation is quite different (nasal vowels, silent letters, liaison).

This library is **spoken-first**: every lesson is built around phrases you'll actually say out loud, not just read.
