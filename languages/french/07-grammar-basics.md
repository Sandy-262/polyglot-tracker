# Lesson 7: Grammar Basics

**Goal:** Understand sentence structure, gendered nouns, and the present tense of the three most common verbs.

## Basic sentence structure

French follows Subject–Verb–Object, just like English: *Je mange une pomme* (I eat an apple). Adjectives usually come **after** the noun: *une pomme rouge* (a red apple) — though a handful of common short adjectives (beau, bon, grand, petit, jeune, vieux) come before: *un bon livre* (a good book).

## Gender & articles

Every French noun is either masculine or feminine — there's no strict rule, so gender is usually memorized along with the word.

| Type | Masculine | Feminine |
|---|---|---|
| "the" (definite) | le (le livre) | la (la table) |
| "a/an" (indefinite) | un (un chat) | une (une maison) |
| before a vowel sound | l' (l'ami) | l' (l'école) |

Plural: **le/la/un/une** all become **les** for "the" (plural) — *les livres*, *les tables*.

## Subject pronouns

| French | English |
|---|---|
| je | I |
| tu | you (informal) |
| il / elle / on | he / she / one (also "we" informally) |
| nous | we |
| vous | you (formal/plural) |
| ils / elles | they (masc./fem.) |

## Être (to be) — present tense

| French | English |
|---|---|
| je suis | I am |
| tu es | you are |
| il/elle/on est | he/she/one is |
| nous sommes | we are |
| vous êtes | you are |
| ils/elles sont | they are |

## Avoir (to have) — present tense

| French | English |
|---|---|
| j'ai | I have |
| tu as | you have |
| il/elle/on a | he/she/one has |
| nous avons | we have |
| vous avez | you have |
| ils/elles ont | they have |

Note: French uses *avoir* where English uses "to be" for age and some feelings: *J'ai vingt ans* (I am 20 — literally "I have 20 years"); *J'ai faim* (I am hungry — "I have hunger").

## Parler (to speak) — a regular -er verb

Most French verbs end in -er and follow this same pattern.

| French | English |
|---|---|
| je parle | I speak |
| tu parles | you speak |
| il/elle/on parle | he/she/one speaks |
| nous parlons | we speak |
| vous parlez | you speak |
| ils/elles parlent | they speak |

## Example sentences

- *Je suis étudiant et j'ai vingt ans.* — I am a student and I am 20 years old.
- *Nous parlons français et anglais.* — We speak French and English.
- *Elle a un chat et un chien.* — She has a cat and a dog.
