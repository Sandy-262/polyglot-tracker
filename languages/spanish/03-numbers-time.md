# Lesson 3: Numbers, Days & Time

**Goal:** Count, tell the date, and tell the time — needed constantly for prices, schedules, and small talk.

## Tens to 100

| Spanish | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| treinta | TRAYN-tah | 30 |
| cuarenta | kwah-REHN-tah | 40 |
| cincuenta | seen-KWEHN-tah | 50 |
| sesenta | seh-SEHN-tah | 60 |
| setenta | seh-TEHN-tah | 70 |
| ochenta | oh-CHEHN-tah | 80 |
| noventa | noh-VEHN-tah | 90 |
| cien | syehn | 100 |

Numbers 21–29 combine as one word (veintiuno, veintidós...). From 31 onward, use "tens + y + unit": **treinta y uno** (31), **cuarenta y cinco** (45).

## Days of the week

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| lunes | Monday |
| martes | Tuesday |
| miércoles | Wednesday |
| jueves | Thursday |
| viernes | Friday |
| sábado | Saturday |
| domingo | Sunday |

Days are not capitalized in Spanish, and the week starts on Monday.

## Months (brief)

enero, febrero, marzo, abril, mayo, junio, julio, agosto, septiembre, octubre, noviembre, diciembre — all lowercase, close to their English cognates.

## Telling time

| Spanish | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| ¿Qué hora es? | keh OH-rah ehs | What time is it? |
| Son las tres | sohn lahs trehs | It's 3 o'clock |
| Es la una | ehs lah OO-nah | It's 1 o'clock (singular!) |
| Son las tres y media | ... ee MEH-dyah | It's 3:30 |
| Son las tres y cuarto | ... ee KWAHR-toh | It's 3:15 |
| Son las cuatro menos cuarto | ... MEH-nohs KWAHR-toh | It's 3:45 (quarter to 4) |
| de la mañana / tarde / noche | — | in the morning/afternoon/night |

## Practice

1. Count out loud from 0 to 20, then by tens to 100.
2. Say today's date and the current time in Spanish.
3. Practice "Son las..." with five random times you make up.
4. Say the days of the week forward and backward without pausing.
