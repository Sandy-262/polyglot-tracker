# Lesson 6: Travel & Directions

**Goal:** Navigate airports, hotels, and streets — ask for and understand directions.

## Transport vocabulary

| Spanish | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| el aeropuerto | ah-eh-roh-PWEHR-toh | the airport |
| la estación | ehs-tah-SYOHN | the station |
| el tren | trehn | the train |
| el autobús | ow-toh-BOOS | the bus |
| el taxi | TAHK-see | the taxi |
| el billete / boleto | bee-YEH-teh / boh-LEH-toh | the ticket |
| la maleta | mah-LEH-tah | the suitcase |
| el vuelo | VWEH-loh | the flight |
| la llegada | yeh-GAH-dah | arrival |
| la salida | sah-LEE-dah | departure / exit |

## Asking for and giving directions

| Spanish | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| ¿Cómo llego a...? | KOH-moh YEH-goh ah | How do I get to...? |
| ¿Está cerca de aquí? | ehs-TAH SEHR-kah deh ah-KEE | Is it near here? |
| Siga todo recto | SEE-gah TOH-doh REHK-toh | Continue straight ahead |
| Doble a la derecha | DOH-bleh ah lah deh-REH-chah | Turn right |
| Está a dos cuadras | ehs-TAH ah dohs KWAH-drahs | It's two blocks away |

## Hotel & airport phrases

| Spanish | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Tengo una reserva | TEHN-goh OO-nah reh-SEHR-vah | I have a reservation |
| ¿A qué hora es el check-out? | ah keh OH-rah ehs... | What time is check-out? |
| ¿Dónde está la puerta de embarque? | DOHN-deh ehs-TAH lah PWEHR-tah deh ehm-BAHR-keh | Where is the boarding gate? |
| Perdí mi equipaje | pehr-DEE mee eh-kee-PAH-heh | I lost my luggage |

## Example dialogue — asking directions

> **Tú:** Disculpe, ¿cómo llego a la estación de tren?
> **Local:** Siga todo recto y doble a la derecha en la esquina.
> **Tú:** ¿Está cerca de aquí?
> **Local:** Sí, está a dos cuadras.
> **Tú:** Muchas gracias.
> **Local:** De nada, ¡buen viaje!

2. Practice giving directions from your home to the nearest shop, in Spanish.
3. Say the hotel check-in phrases as if you just arrived.
4. Describe a lost-luggage situation at an airport using the vocabulary above.
