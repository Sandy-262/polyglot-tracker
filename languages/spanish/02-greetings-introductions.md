# Lesson 2: Greetings & Introductions

**Goal:** Confidently greet someone, introduce yourself, and ask basic questions about them — the first 60 seconds of any conversation.

## Basic greetings

| Spanish | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Hola | OH-lah | Hello / Hi |
| Buenos días | BWEH-nohs DEE-ahs | Good morning |
| Buenas tardes | BWEH-nahs TAHR-dehs | Good afternoon |
| Buenas noches | BWEH-nahs NOH-chehs | Good evening / night |
| ¿Qué tal? | keh tahl | How's it going? (casual) |
| ¿Cómo estás? | KOH-moh ehs-TAHS | How are you? (informal) |
| ¿Cómo está usted? | KOH-moh ehs-TAH oos-TEHD | How are you? (formal) |
| Bien, gracias | byehn GRAH-syahs | Fine, thanks |
| Más o menos | mahs oh MEH-nohs | So-so |
| Adiós | ah-DYOHS | Goodbye |
| Hasta luego | AHS-tah LWEH-goh | See you later |
| Hasta mañana | AHS-tah mah-NYAH-nah | See you tomorrow |

## Introducing yourself

| Spanish | Pronunciation guide | English |
|---|---|---|
| Me llamo... | meh YAH-moh | My name is... |
| ¿Cómo te llamas? | KOH-moh teh YAH-mahs | What's your name? (informal) |
| ¿Cómo se llama usted? | KOH-moh seh YAH-mah oos-TEHD | What's your name? (formal) |
| Mucho gusto | MOO-choh GOOS-toh | Nice to meet you |
| Encantado / Encantada | ehn-kahn-TAH-doh/dah | Delighted (male/female speaker) |
| Soy de... | soy deh | I'm from... |
| ¿De dónde eres? | deh DOHN-deh EH-rehs | Where are you from? (informal) |
| Tengo ___ años | TEHN-goh ... AH-nyohs | I am ___ years old |

## Formal vs informal: tú vs usted

Spanish has two ways to say "you," and picking the right one matters:

- **Tú** — informal, used with friends, family, peers, children.
- **Usted** — formal, used with strangers, elders, authority figures, or in business.

When in doubt (especially in Spain and much of Latin America), start with **usted** and switch to **tú** once the other person invites it or uses it with you first. Latin American countries vary in how strictly this is observed — Spain and the Andes tend to be more formal than Central America.

## Example dialogue

> **A:** ¡Hola! Buenos días.
> **B:** ¡Buenos días! ¿Cómo estás?
> **A:** Bien, gracias. ¿Y tú?
> **B:** Muy bien. Me llamo Carlos, ¿y tú?
> **A:** Me llamo Ana. Mucho gusto.
> **B:** Encantado, Ana. ¿De dónde eres?
> **A:** Soy de India. ¿Y tú?
> **B:** Soy de México.

## Practice

1. Say the full dialogue above out loud, playing both parts.
2. Introduce yourself using "Me llamo," "Soy de," and "Tengo ___ años" — all in one breath.
3. Practice the same introduction twice: once with tú, once with usted, changing the question forms accordingly.
4. Record yourself greeting someone at each time of day (morning/afternoon/evening).
