# Malayalam (മലയാളം)

Malayalam is a Dravidian language spoken by roughly **35 million people**, mainly in Kerala, India, and by diaspora communities worldwide. Coming from Italian, Spanish or French, expect two big adjustments: a new script (one of the most complex in the world, with many conjunct consonants) and **SOV (subject-object-verb)** word order instead of SVO. Both are covered explicitly in Lessons 1 and 7.

## Suggested study plan

- **~2 lessons/week over 4 weeks** to get through the core library once.
- Revisit **Lesson 8** weekly as an ongoing speaking-practice anchor, even after finishing the rest.
- Audio resources for Malayalam are thinner online than for Italian/Spanish/French — lean on [Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/)'s text-to-speech and [Forvo](https://forvo.com) for pronunciation checks, but prioritize practicing with native speakers (family, friends, or a language exchange app like Tandem/HelloTalk) whenever you can — it will move you forward faster than any recording.
