# Lesson 8: Conversation Practice

**Goal:** Combine lessons 2–6 into one natural conversation, then practice speaking out loud.

## Full dialogue: meeting, chatting, eating, and finding the way

> **Arun:** നമസ്കാരം! സുഖമാണോ?
> *(namaskāram! sukhamāṇō?)* — Hello! How are you?
>
> **Priya:** സുഖമാണ്, നന്ദി. എന്റെ പേര് പ്രിയ. നിങ്ങളുടെ പേരെന്താണ്?
> *(sukhamāṇŭ, nandi. ente pēru priya. niṅṅaḷuṭe pēr enthāṇŭ?)* — I'm fine, thanks. My name is Priya. What's your name?
>
> **Arun:** എന്റെ പേര് അരുൺ. സന്തോഷം.
> *(ente pēru aruṇ. santhōṣham.)* — My name is Arun. Nice to meet you.
>
> **Priya:** നമുക്ക് എന്തെങ്കിലും കഴിക്കാം. ഹോട്ടൽ അടുത്താണോ?
> *(namukku enthenkilum kaḻikkām. hōṭṭal aṭuththāṇō?)* — Let's eat something. Is the restaurant nearby?
>
> **Arun:** അതെ, നേരെ പോയിട്ട് വലത്തേക്ക് തിരിയുക.
> *(athe, nēre pōyiṭṭŭ valaththēkku thiriyuka.)* — Yes, go straight and turn right.
>
> *(at the restaurant)*
>
> **Waiter:** എന്താണ് വേണ്ടത്?
> *(enthāṇŭ vēṇṭathŭ?)* — What would you like?
>
> **Priya:** എനിക്ക് ഒരു ദോശയും ചായയും വേണം. ഞാൻ വെജിറ്റേറിയൻ ആണ്.
> *(enikku oru dōśayum chāyayum vēṇam. ñān vejiṭeriyan āṇŭ.)* — I'd like a dosa and tea. I'm vegetarian.
>
> **Arun:** ബിൽ തരുമോ?
> *(bil tharumō?)* — Could we have the bill?
>
> **Priya:** വളരെ നന്ദി! വീണ്ടും കാണാം.
> *(vaḷare nandi! vīṇṭum kāṇām.)* — Thank you very much! See you again.

## Role-play prompts

Try these out loud, alone or with a partner — record yourself if you can:

1. **Meeting someone new:** Greet a stranger, introduce yourself, and ask where they're from.
2. **At a restaurant:** Order two dishes from Lesson 5, mention a dietary restriction, and ask for the bill.
3. **Lost in a new city:** Ask a stranger for directions to the train station, thank them, and ask if they speak English in case you get confused.

## What's next

- **Practice with native speakers** is the single best way to progress in Malayalam — recorded audio resources are thinner here than for Italian/Spanish/French, so real conversation matters more. If you have Malayalam-speaking family or friends, speak with them often, even just a few lines a day.
- Try a language-exchange app like **Tandem** or **HelloTalk** to find native Malayalam speakers who want to practice your language in return.
- Revisit this lesson weekly and try extending the dialogue yourself — swap in your own name, food preferences, and destinations.

## Practice

1. Perform the full dialogue out loud, both roles, at least twice.
2. Record yourself doing all 3 role-plays and listen back for pronunciation gaps.
3. Write and speak one new sentence of your own for each of the 6 topic areas covered (greetings, numbers, phrases, food, travel, grammar).
