# Lesson 5: Food & Dining

**Goal:** Order food, discuss dietary needs, and navigate a Kerala restaurant.

## Restaurant basics

| Malayalam script | Transliteration | English phonetic | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| ഹോട്ടൽ | hōṭṭal | HOT-tal | restaurant (colloquial — "hotel" means eatery in Kerala) |
| മെനു | menu | MEH-noo | menu |
| എനിക്ക് ... വേണം | enikku ... vēṇam | eh-NIK-ku VAY-num | I want ... |
| ഒരു ... തരുമോ? | oru ... tharumō? | OH-roo thuh-ru-MO | could I have a ...? |
| ബിൽ തരുമോ? | bil tharumō? | bill thuh-ru-MO | could I have the bill? |
| ഇത് നല്ല രുചിയാണ് | ithŭ nalla ruchiyāṇŭ | I-thu NUL-luh ru-chi-YAA-nu | this tastes good |
| എരിവ് കൂടുതലാണ് | erivŭ kūṭuthalāṇŭ | EH-ri-vu koo-du-thuh-LAA-nu | it's too spicy |
| വെള്ളം തരുമോ? | veḷḷam tharumō? | VEL-lum thuh-ru-MO | could I have water? |

## Mini restaurant dialogue

> **Waiter:** നമസ്കാരം, എന്താണ് വേണ്ടത്?
> *(namaskāram, enthāṇŭ vēṇṭathŭ?)* — Hello, what would you like?
>
> **You:** എനിക്ക് ഒരു ദോശയും ഒരു കാപ്പിയും വേണം.
> *(enikku oru dōśayum oru kāppiyum vēṇam.)* — I'd like a dosa and a coffee.
>
> **Waiter:** വെജിറ്റേറിയൻ ആണോ?
> *(vejiṭeriyan āṇō?)* — Are you vegetarian?
>
> **You:** അതെ, ഞാൻ വെജിറ്റേറിയൻ ആണ്.
> *(athe, ñān vejiṭeriyan āṇŭ.)* — Yes, I'm vegetarian.
>
> **You (later):** ബിൽ തരുമോ?
> *(bil tharumō?)* — Could I have the bill?

## Practice

1. Order your 3 favorite foods from the dish list above out loud, as full sentences.
2. Practice saying you're vegetarian/allergic to something relevant to you.
3. Role-play the mini-dialogue, swapping in different dishes.
4. Ask for the bill and say thank you in one breath.
